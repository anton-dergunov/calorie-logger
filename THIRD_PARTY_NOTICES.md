# Third-Party Data Notices

## Open Food Facts

Packaged-food search results are provided by [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/).

- The database is available under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
- Individual database contents are available under the [Database Contents License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/).
- Calorie Logger does not download or redistribute Open Food Facts product images.

Open Food Facts data is contributed by its community and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Calorie Logger asks users to review imported nutrition before saving it.

## ZXing WebAssembly barcode reader

Camera barcode decoding uses the reader-only build of [zxing-wasm](https://github.com/Sec-ant/zxing-wasm) when the browser does not provide compatible native barcode detection.

- zxing-wasm-specific code is licensed under the MIT License.
- ZXing-C++ and the WebAssembly bindings are licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
- Bundled zint components are licensed under the BSD 3-Clause License.

The matching WebAssembly binary is bundled with Calorie Logger and is never fetched from a CDN.

## CoFID 2021

Generic-food results contain public sector information from [McCance and Widdowson’s Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset 2021](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid), originally published by Public Health England.

Contains public sector information licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

Calorie Logger bundles a reduced, normalized extract containing food identifiers, names, descriptions, units, calories, protein, fat, and carbohydrate values. It excludes the separately published unreviewed “old foods” dataset.
